# Source: Big Four Document Design & Intaglio Engraving Engine

- **Type:** Design Specification & Aesthetic Standard
- **Origin / Author:** Bhupesh Chandra Dimri
- **File:** `raw/articles/big-four-intaglio-engraving-design-engine.md`
- **Date Ingested:** 2026-11-14

## Key Takeaways
1. **Convergence of Consulting Rigor & Banknote Craftsmanship:** Synthesizes the institutional credibility of Big Four reporting (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG) with the visual authority of central bank currency engraving and high-security financial certificates.
2. **Vector-Based Intaglio Guilloché Engine:** Employs mathematical curves (hypotrochoids and epitrochoids) to render resolution-independent ornamental borders, concentric rosettes, corner flourishes, and micro-line dividers.
3. **Subtle Watermark & Coordinate Background:** Uses low-opacity, light-ink density background textures (topographic lines, coordinate grids, radial rosettes) that elevate prestige without reducing typography legibility.
4. **Institutional Color Palette:** Standardizes on Midnight Navy (`#14243A`), Deep Charcoal (`#252B33`), Antique Gold (`#B49A62`), Engraved Silver (`#A7ADB5`), Ivory Paper (`#F7F5EF`), and Muted Blue (`#647C91`).
5. **Print-Ready Typography & Layout:** Leverages authoritative serif headings, high-readability sans-serif prose, monospaced GPS coordinates, and structured sectioning across multi-page intelligence briefings.
