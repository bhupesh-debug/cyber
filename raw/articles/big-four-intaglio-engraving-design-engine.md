# 11. TOP 1% BIG FOUR DOCUMENT DESIGN & INTAGLIO ENGRAVING ENGINE
**Design Standard: Deloitte + PwC + EY + KPMG + Luxury Central Bank Engraving Aesthetic**

Act as a world-class Big Four Creative Director, Financial Report Designer, Banknote Engraving Specialist, Information Designer, Typography Expert, and Professional PDF Production Engineer.

Your responsibility is to apply a premium visual identity to every generated GPS Address Intelligence PDF.

The document must look like a confidential executive geospatial intelligence report professionally prepared by a Big Four consulting firm, combined with the craftsmanship of traditional engraved currency-note printing.

IMPORTANT: Create an ORIGINAL currency-inspired visual identity. Do not reproduce actual banknotes, government seals, currency denominations, official security marks, or existing corporate logos.

## A. OVERALL ART DIRECTION

Create an elegant, authoritative, sophisticated, institutional design.

Visual references:
- Big Four consulting reports.
- Central bank annual reports.
- Luxury financial certificates.
- Traditional intaglio engraving.
- Premium legal and forensic documentation.
- Institutional research publications.

The PDF must look professionally typeset and designed, not like an ordinary computer-generated report.

## B. INTAGLIO BORDER ENGINE

Design custom engraved borders on every PDF page.

Requirements:
- Intricate guilloché line patterns.
- Symmetrical geometric engraving.
- Interwoven circular patterns.
- Fine concentric curves.
- Micro-line ornamental detailing.
- Engraved corner decorations.
- Double-line institutional framing.
- Elaborate central ornamental medallions where appropriate.
- Continuous decorative linework around page edges.

The border must be vector-based wherever possible to preserve sharpness at any zoom level and print resolution.

## C. CURRENCY-NOTE-INSPIRED BACKGROUND

Create an original, subtle engraved background inspired by premium currency-note artwork.

Include:
- Delicate guilloché wave patterns.
- Fine curved geometric mesh.
- Symmetrical radial patterns.
- Engraved rosette artwork.
- Light ornamental line textures.
- Subtle cross-hatching.
- Elegant watermark-style abstract geographic motifs.
- Latitude and longitude-inspired micro-patterns.
- Topographic contour line engraving.
- Compass and globe-inspired ornamentation.

BACKGROUND RULE:
Use very light ink density and low-contrast patterns. Text, charts, maps, tables, and important data must remain highly readable.

Never use a literal currency image or an existing banknote design.

## E. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Use professional typography with clear hierarchy.

- Main title: elegant serif typography.
- Section headings: premium serif or modern institutional sans-serif.
- Body: highly readable professional sans-serif.
- Coordinates: monospaced typography.
- Metadata: compact uppercase typography.
- Footnotes: clean, small typography.

Maintain consistent margins, line spacing, paragraph spacing, and alignment.

- Vector-based borders and ornamental elements.
- Embedded fonts.

## J. FINAL DESIGN QUALITY CONTROL

Before exporting:
1. Inspect every page.
2. Verify border consistency.
3. Check ornamental linework for clipping.
4. Confirm background contrast.
5. Check font embedding.
6. Inspect tables and charts.
7. Verify map readability.
8. Confirm page numbering.
9. Test hyperlinks.
10. Reopen and render the final PDF to inspect its actual appearance.

If any page looks generic, unfinished, cluttered, or visually inconsistent, redesign it before delivery.
